Play together.

You’re always in the same room. Select Join game and enter the password. The first person is Player 1, and the next is Player 2. Once you’re both here, either player can choose a minigame. Use All games to return to the shelf and resume later.

In a Nutshell

The Nut Picker sees a full question and its answer. Tap words to hide them, keep at least one revealed, and lock the words. The other player types one answer. If correct, the Nut Picker earns 1 point per hidden word; otherwise 0. Answers ignore case and punctuation. Swap roles each round. All 50 original questions are shuffled, with no repeats until the deck runs out. Restart scores resets this game only.

Hues & Cues

  1. Pick a color. The clue giver gets four different swatches. Shuffle them, then choose one in secret.
  2. One word. Send a one-word hint. Your partner picks a square and locks in their first guess.
  3. One or two words. Send a second hint. Your partner locks in a second guess.
  4. The big reveal. Each guess earns 3 for exact, 2 within one square, or 1 within two squares. Diagonals count. Points go to the guesser.

Swap roles each round. Keep playing as long as you like. Restart scores to start fresh.

Hyphens separate words. Scores stay while the server runs. Use separate browsers, devices, or private windows to play both seats on one computer.
